# Design system

## Visual hierarchy

1. Faithful source identity and original title.
2. Dominant nail-focused crop.
3. Supporting angle or detail crop.
4. Short editorial observation.
5. Small decorative material cues.

The collage should feel assembled by an editor, not filled by a template engine. Vary main-image height, secondary-image overlap, negative-space balance, text position, and ornament scale across a batch.

## Palette method

Sample colors from the manicure itself, not from the app UI or surrounding clothing.

- `background`: mix the dominant nail color toward white until it is low-saturation and quiet.
- `ink`: darken a supporting manicure color enough for readable text.
- `accent`: preserve a recognizable supporting hue at medium lightness.
- Avoid a fixed pink, beige, or warm-white default.
- If two manicures have similar colors, separate them through materials and temperature: ice versus pearl, coffee versus tortoiseshell, fog versus sage.

## Material-theme rule

Choose materials that explain the finish:

| Nail cue | Possible material language |
| --- | --- |
| silver cat-eye, transparent glitter | crystal, moon, glass bead, silver tulle |
| caramel nude, amber glaze | resin, maple leaf, coffee ceramic, velvet strip |
| pearl white, shell shine | mother-of-pearl, pearl strand, sea glass, feather |
| tortoiseshell, gold detail | acetate, antique chain, dried chrysanthemum, velvet tag |
| smoky nude, rhinestone | quartz, brooch, champagne lace, gold pin |
| milky iridescence | bubbles, opal orb, organza ruffle, sugar pearls |
| dusty blue with warm brown | pressed blue flower, ochre leaf, gold foil, blue-gray ribbon curl |
| gray-green, muted pink-brown | sage, bellflower, cocoa paper, metallic dew |

These are examples, not a mandatory catalog. Inspect the current batch and avoid repeating its hero materials.

## Composition rules

- Default canvas: 1440 x 1920 portrait.
- Upper-left card: roughly 55–62% of canvas width; include authentic author bar and title strip.
- Lower-right image: roughly 45–55% of canvas width; overlap the main card intentionally.
- Use straight editorial edges unless the reference calls for another treatment.
- Leave at least one broad quiet region for short notes and a small doodle.
- Do not add a third nail photo unless the user requests it.
- Do not place stickers over nail plates, signature details, creator names, or required watermarks.

## Copy style

Keep commentary observational and source-grounded. Two short lines are enough. Describe visible color, texture, finish, proportion, or balance; do not invent wear tests, salon results, prices, products, or personal experience.

## QA checklist

- [ ] Exact source author and title retained.
- [ ] Main and secondary crops both add information.
- [ ] Nail detail remains the focal point.
- [ ] Background and accents are sampled from this manicure.
- [ ] Hero sticker/material is unique within the batch.
- [ ] Decoration does not cover nails or credits.
- [ ] No engagement counts or copied comment UI.
- [ ] Editorial copy is visually separate from source copy.
- [ ] Rights status and unresolved issues appear in the manifest.
