# Rights and credits

Publicly visible or user-supplied content is not automatically licensed.

Before public or commercial publication, verify as applicable:

- copyright permission for source photographs, screenshots, illustrations, music, and decorative assets;
- personality or portrait rights for identifiable people;
- trademark and false-affiliation risk;
- permission for edits, commercial use, channel, territory, and duration;
- platform requirements for AI-generated or AI-edited media;
- factual claims and original creator attribution.

Attribution, noncommercial intent, deletion on request, or AI redrawing does not by itself resolve permission. Keep original creator credits separate from `layout`, `editorial copy`, or `curation` credits.

For a public GitHub repository, include only original, licensed, or synthetic demo media. Do not ship scraped posts, user cookies, private tokens, or third-party screenshots as example assets.
